# Admin onboarding

## Roles

- **Admin:** orders, approved customer contact, student review, schedule, catalogue, invoice/payment review, notices and status updates.
- **Super Admin:** all Admin work plus staff grants, security settings, retention controls and explicitly authorised destructive/anonymisation workflows.

## First-day checklist

1. Create a unique staff account and enrol the approved MFA method.
2. Confirm the assigned role using the least-privilege rule; shared accounts are prohibited.
3. Practise finding an order, reading customer-safe versus internal notes and appending a status without modifying history.
4. Practise student verification: view evidence through a short-lived signed link, record decision/reason and close the signed view.
5. Practise payment review: proof moves to `PAYMENT_REVIEW`; independent evidence is required before authorised staff select `PAID`.
6. Practise a weight adjustment, customer approval and the difference between estimate, invoice and verified revenue.
7. Review CSV export handling. Export only the minimum fields, store it temporarily in an approved encrypted location and dispose of it under the approved retention policy.
8. Confirm incident escalation, account-revocation contact and who may publish notices or policy versions.

## Never

Never download student ID or proof to a personal device, paste sensitive data into WhatsApp, mark proof as paid automatically, share signed links, rewrite history, hard-delete normal orders or publish a draft policy/contact channel.
